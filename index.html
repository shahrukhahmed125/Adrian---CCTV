What areas do you cover for installations?

We cover Birmingham, the wider West Midlands and surrounding areas. We install home and business security systems you can rely on, right across the region.

How long does a typical installation take?

Most residential security system installations finish within one day. The exact time depends on the size of your property and the equipment you choose.

Do you handle larger business properties?

Yes. Our commercial security installation services cover offices, retail shops, warehouses and rental properties of all sizes across the region.

What warranty do you offer on your work?

We give a three-year warranty on parts and a one-year warranty on labour, for complete peace of mind.

Can I monitor my property from my phone?

Yes. Most systems we install connect to your smartphone, so you can check your cameras and alarm whenever you like.

How do I find a trusted installer?

Before you book, look for proper reviews and a clear warranty policy. ADI Security Installations offers both, along with honest advice and a free, no-obligation quote.
